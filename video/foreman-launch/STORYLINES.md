# Storylines

## A. Two laptops, one function (recommended, built)

The story a stranger follows best: two people they can picture, one function they both want.

**Device and form:** one object travels through the film (`validateToken`, held, wanted, handed over), told as a split screen that collapses to one.

The viewer sees two laptops side by side: Rishikhesh (blue) and Joel-Marc (orange). They join one Foreman room, their agents work in the same file, one is made to wait, one gets warned, and the function changes hands with the work already merged. It feels calm, as if a hard coordination problem quietly solved itself.

- **First 2 s:** two VS Code windows side by side, both Claudes typing into `validateToken` at the same moment.
- **Beats:** hook (two agents, one file) → tension (merge conflict) → reveal (Foreman) → join (Start a room, code, Join, Allow) → proof (function locks, refusal, caller warning) → payoff (handoff, colour flips, merged) → claim (zero merge conflicts) → end card.
- **Look:** the product's own world (VS Code dark `#1E1E1E` UI at 1.6× size), brand blue `#378ADD` colour floods between worlds, a white end card. Blue, orange and amber are the people and the warning.
- **Sound:** silent for this cut (no music source signed in). Plan: a minimal pulse that holds back until the handoff, a soft lock click on each lock, and one riser into the colour flip.

## B. The function's passport

**Device and form:** the function block is the camera's subject, in one continuous take.

The camera never leaves `validateToken`. Stamps land on it (locked by Joel-Marc, waiting: Rishikhesh, warning: callers), and the take ends as it is handed over and its stripe flips colour. Intimate and clever, but it hides the "two laptops" fact, which is the whole point.

## C. Type carries the argument

**Device and form:** kinetic type cut on the beat, with product shots as the punctuation.

Giant words carry the line: "Two agents. One function. Zero merge conflicts." Each word hands off to a half-second product shot. Bold and fast, but a viewer sees less of how it works.
